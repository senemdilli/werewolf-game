The game is Werewolf, a social deduction game with 8 players and these roles:

- 2 Werewolves — at night they secretly agree on one player to kill.
- 1 Seer — each night learns the faction (village or werewolf) of one player.
- 1 Witch — after the werewolves act, may use one healing potion (revive that
  night's victim) and/or one poison (kill a player); one of each for the whole
  game. It is not revealed whether a death was caused by werewolves or the witch.
- 4 Villagers — no special ability.

Only the werewolves know each other's identities. Everyone else knows only their
own role.

Win conditions:
- The village wins when all werewolves are eliminated.
- The werewolves win when their number is equal to or greater than the number of
  remaining villagers.

Day cycle:
1. Morning: the deaths from the night are announced. Dead players' roles are
   revealed. If there is no mayor, an election is held.
2. Discussion: players talk about who might be a werewolf.
3. Evening: all living players vote to exile one player; the exiled player's role
   is revealed.

Mayor: elected by a vote among all living players; the mayor's vote counts double.
Individual mayor votes are secret — only the elected mayor is announced. If the
mayor dies, a new one is elected.

Night sequence: Seer investigates, then Werewolves choose a victim, then the Witch
may act.
